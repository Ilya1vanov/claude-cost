Compact threshold

The context size above which compacting costs less than carrying on, learned per model from your own history. A compaction costs summary output plus writing the tokens it leaves, at the 75th percentile of your observed compactions; a model with fewer than 20 compactions uses the global threshold. Carrying a context is priced at the cache-read price.
